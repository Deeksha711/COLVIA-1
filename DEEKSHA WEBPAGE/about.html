ABOUT COLVIA

Your Future. Your Choices. One Smart Guide.

COLVIA (College + Via) is a student-focused platform designed to make college and course discovery simpler and more personalized. Instead of searching through lengthy college databases and scattered information, COLVIA brings relevant options together based on a student's academic profile, interests and preferences.

HOW COLVIA WORKS

From profile to possibilities.

01

Build Your Profile

Students provide relevant academic details, entrance-exam information, career interests, course interests and preferences.

02

Get Personalized Options

COLVIA uses the student's profile and preferences to present colleges and courses that are more relevant to their requirements.

03

Check Eligibility

Students can check the eligibility requirements associated with the colleges and courses they are interested in.

04

Compare Colleges

Students can compare selected colleges using important factors such as courses, fees, eligibility, location and facilities.

WHY COLVIA

Making college research more focused.

Finding the right college can involve going through large amounts of information from different sources. COLVIA aims to make this process easier by turning that information into a more focused set of choices based on the student's own profile and preferences.

COLVIA does not make the decision for the student. It helps students explore, compare and understand their options so they can make a more informed choice.

INFORMATION

Built around relevant information.

COLVIA focuses on providing useful college and course information in a simple and accessible format. Wherever possible, information is connected with authentic college and official sources so students have a reliable starting point for their research.
